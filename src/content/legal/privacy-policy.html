Introduction

Level Up Cincinnati, Inc. ("Level Up Cincinnati," "Level Up," "we," "us," or "our") is a non-profit centered on supporting first-generation college students by offering coaching, support, and personal development. We operate the Level Up Cincinnati website at https://www.levelupcincinnati.org/ (the "Website"), a web application at https://app.levelupcincinnati.org/, and the Level Up Cincinnati mobile application available on the Apple App Store and Google Play Store (the "Mobile App"). Our non-profit services, website, web application, mobile application, or other services or content we may offer are collectively the "Services". We are committed to respecting the privacy of information and data of the users of our Services.

This policy describes our policies and practices with respect to the collection and use of information that's about you (your "Personal Information") when you give it to us, visit our Website, use our Mobile App, donate to us, or use any Services. This information can be your name, email address, your contact information, and anything else that tells someone something about you. If you do not agree to the terms set forth herein, please do not use our Services. We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time, and the revision date above provides you with notice that the Privacy Policy has been updated. This Privacy Policy applies regardless of whether you use our Services via a computer, mobile device, tablet, or other medium (collectively, your "Equipment").

Personal Information We Collect and How We Collect It

We collect several types of Personal Information from and about users of our Services, including:

Identity Information. Examples include your first name and last name, birthday, mailing address, email address, phone number, photograph, or similar identifiers.

Employment Information. Examples include your current job history or job title, your academic background, academic transcripts, and pre-employment screening and background check information (which may include criminal records information).

Demographic Information. Examples include your age, marital and family status, number of children, income level, languages spoken, household demographic data, data from social media platforms, education and professional information, hobbies and interests and propensity scores from third parties (likelihood of donation, experiencing a life event, etc.).

Communication Information. Examples include the communications you send us, your feedback that you communicate to us, and similar communication between you and us via email, social media, or any other medium.

Technical Information. Examples include your Equipment's hardware information, operating system, platform information, browser type, language information, browser plugin types, IP address, browsing activities and patterns, and similar technical information.

Usage Information. Examples include information about how you use our Services.

Financial Information. Examples include details on payment cards or other forms of payment.

Transaction Information. Examples include details about donations and other payments you made to us.

Profile Information. Examples include your interests, your coach-student relationship, your college major and graduation year, your LinkedIn profile, your college experience, and similar information about your interests, preferences, feedback and responses with Level Up Cincinnati.

Sensitive Personal Information, such as your racial or ethnic origin, if you opt in to share such information. For more information, see the section Sensitive Personal Information below.

We collect this information:

  • Directly from you when you provide it to us, including through our Mobile App or web application.
  • Automatically as you navigate through the site or use our Mobile App. Information collected automatically may include usage details, IP addresses, and information collected through cookies, web beacons, and other tracking technologies.
  • From third parties including, for example, our affiliated companies, business partners, subcontractors, and service providers. We may also receive information from publicly available sources such as from social networking sites.

Mobile Application

When you use our Mobile App, we may collect the following additional information:

  • Camera and Photo Library. With your permission, we access your device's camera and photo library to allow you to take or upload a profile photo. We do not access your camera or photos for any other purpose.
  • Push Notification Tokens. We may collect device tokens to send you notifications about events, program updates, and other relevant information. You can manage notification preferences through your device settings.
  • Profile Photos. Profile photos you upload are stored in Firebase Cloud Storage and are visible to other authenticated program participants.

Our Mobile App uses Google Firebase for authentication, data storage, and file storage. Your data is processed and stored by Firebase in accordance with Google's security and privacy practices. For more information, see Firebase Privacy and Security.

Examples of how we may use the information we collect include:

  • Manage our relationship with you which may include notifying you about changes to our Services, Terms of Use, and/or Privacy Policy, providing you notices, asking you to provide feedback or complete a survey, or addressing any concerns you may have about our Services.

  • Respond to questions or comments from you.
  • Provide support to our students, coaches, and donors as applicable.
  • Plan and manage our contractual relationships with our vendors.
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical issues.
  • Complete internal analysis of information enabling us to monitor the usage of our Services.
  • Improve our Services.
  • Target our Services and efforts to the highest need.
  • Determine student eligibility for our Services and prospective coach eligibility for volunteering as a Level Up coach.
  • Facilitate strong and effective coach-student relationships and gauge the progress and wellbeing of our students.
  • Track the impact of our charitable efforts.
  • Address legal issues, including (i) complying with our obligations to retain certain business records; (ii) establishing, exercising, or defending legal claims; (iii) complying with laws, regulations, court orders, or other legal processes; (iv) detecting and preventing fraud or intellectual property infringement claims, violations of our contracts or agreements, violations of law, or other misuses of our Services; and (v) protecting our rights or property, or yours or others' health, safety, welfare, rights, or property.
  • Allow you to exercise your data privacy rights.

In addition, we may process your personal data for the following reasons:

  • To carry out our obligations and enforce our rights.
  • In any other way we may describe when you provide the information.
  • For any other purpose with your express consent.

We may use information that is not Personal Information for any purpose. For example, we may use aggregated data such as statistical data or aggregate demographic data for any purpose ("Aggregated Data"). Aggregated Data could be derived from your Personal Information, but Aggregated Data is not considered Personal Information, as this data will not directly or indirectly reveal your identity. For example, we may aggregate your usage data to calculate the percentage of users accessing our Services. However, if we combine or connect Aggregated Data with your Personal Information so that it can directly or indirectly identify you, we treat the combined data as Personal Information which will be used in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

Cookies and Automatic Data Collection Technologies

Our Services may use automatic data collection technologies to distinguish you from other users. It also allows us to improve our Services by enabling us to:

  • Estimate our audience size and usage patterns.
  • Store your preferences, so we may customize our Services according to your individual interests.
  • Recognize you when you return to our Services.

The technologies we use for this automatic data collection may include:

  • Cookies (or browser cookies). A cookie is a small file placed on the hard drive of your computer. For information about managing browser settings to refuse cookies, see Your Rights and Choices.
  • Flash Cookies. Certain features of our website may use Flash cookies (local stored objects) instead of browser cookies to collect and store information about your preferences and navigation to, from, and on the website. For information about managing Flash cookies, see Your Rights and Choices.
  • Web Beacons, Pixel Tags, Clear Gifs. Our website pages and emails may contain small transparent embedded images or objects known as web beacons (also referred to as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs) that permit us, for example, to count website page visitors or email readers, or to compile other similar statistics such as recording website content popularity or verifying system and server integrity. For information about managing web beacons, see Your Rights and Choices.

It is your responsibility to protect your login information used in connection with the Services, and you should not share your password.
