# Level Up Cincinnati: Outreach Playbook

A plain-English guide for staff and volunteers. It covers how to get more coaches, volunteers, sponsors, and donors to find the new website, and how to tell what's working. You don't need any technical skills. Most steps take 15–30 minutes, once.

---

## 1. Google Search Console (do this first, the week the new site launches)

Search Console shows how people find you on Google, and tells Google about the new site.

1. Go to <https://search.google.com/search-console> and sign in with the Level Up Google account.
2. Click **Add property**, choose **Domain**, and enter `levelupcincinnati.org`. Google shows a TXT record. Add it at your domain registrar (the same place the Netlify DNS records go), then click **Verify**.
3. **Submit the sitemap.** Left menu → **Sitemaps** → enter `sitemap-index.xml` → **Submit**.
4. **Clean out the old junk URLs.** The old Squarespace sitemap listed dozens of coach and scholar pages with long garbled addresses (like `/level-up-coaches-directory/stacyrodarte-gy7bh-…`).
   - The new site **permanently redirects every one of them** to the right person's new page, so Google will clean them up on its own over a few weeks.
   - To speed it up: **Indexing → Pages**, find any old URLs, and click **Validate fix**.
   - Only use the **Removals** tool for a page that should disappear from Google entirely.
5. Check back monthly under **Performance → Search results**. Look at which searches bring people in, especially "mentor," "volunteer," and "first-generation."

## 2. Google Business Profile (free map and search listing)

People searching "volunteer mentor Cincinnati" often see map results first.

1. Go to <https://business.google.com> → **Add business** → name it **Level Up Cincinnati** → category **Non-profit organization** (add **Educational consultant** as a second category if it's offered).
2. Level Up uses a PO Box, so choose **"I don't have a location customers can visit"** and set the **service area** to Cincinnati, Northern Kentucky, and Hamilton County.
3. Add:
   - website `https://www.levelupcincinnati.org`
   - phone 513-549-2779
   - email hello@levelupcincinnati.org
4. Upload 10+ real photos: Match Day, Aiming for Opportunity, coaches with scholars. They're all in the website's `src/assets/img` folder.
5. **Post an update every time there's an event** (HERizon, Aiming for Opportunity). Posts show on Google Search and Maps.
6. Ask coaches and partners to leave a Google review. Share the review link from the profile.

## 3. Google Ad Grants: $10,000/month in free search ads

Eligible 501(c)(3)s get up to **$10,000 per month** in free Google search ads.

**Apply:**
1. Register with **Google for Nonprofits**: <https://www.google.com/nonprofits>. Verification goes through Percent, and you'll need the EIN 88-0931411.
2. Once approved, activate **Google Ad Grants** from the Google for Nonprofits dashboard and complete the short eligibility check. It confirms the website has HTTPS, substantial content, and clear calls to action; the new site meets all of these.
3. Build the account in **Expert mode** using the three starter ad groups below.

- Keep a 5% click-through rate.
- No single-word keywords, except your brand name.
- Track at least one conversion. Section 7 sets these up.

## 5. LinkedIn posting rhythm

LinkedIn is where coaches, sponsors, and partners already are.

| When | What | Link to |
|---|---|---|
| **Weekly** (Tue–Thu mornings) | One post: a photo plus 3–5 sentences | Rotate: Become a Coach, Events, Donate |
| **Monthly** | Scholar or coach story (see section 6) | `/become-a-coach` or `/students` |
| **Before each event** | Announcement 3 weeks out, reminder 1 week out, recap with photos the day after | The event page |
| **Twice a year** (before summer coach recruiting and before Aiming for Opportunity) | "We're recruiting coaches / sponsors" push. Ask board members and coaches to reshare. | `/become-a-coach`, `/aiming-for-opportunity` |

Tips:
- Tag partner companies and coaches' employers (with permission). This is the biggest reach booster.
- Ask every new coach to add **"Volunteer Coach, Level Up Cincinnati"** to their LinkedIn experience.

## 6. One story a month

Stories recruit coaches better than statistics.

**Format:**
1. One photo of a scholar and coach together.
2. A quote from each.
3. One concrete result: an internship, Dean's List, a first networking event.
4. A closing line with a single ask, such as "We need coaches for this summer's class → levelupcincinnati.org/become-a-coach".

**Before you post:**
- Get written OK from the scholar, and the coach, before posting names or photos.
- Use first names for current students unless they say otherwise.

**Where to post:** LinkedIn, Instagram, the mailing list, and the Google Business Profile.

**Story ideas already on the site:** Tricia & Stephanie, Martha & Elena, and Nick & Stuart. Follow up with them for an "update" story.

## 7. Tracking what works

Track three numbers every month in a simple spreadsheet:

| What | Where to find it |
|---|---|
| **Coach applications** | Netlify → Forms → `coach-application` (count per month) |
| **Volunteer signups** | volunteer.levelupcincinnati.org admin |
| **Donations** | Givebutter → Transactions (filter by month) |
| Also useful | Netlify Forms `partner-inquiry`, `launch-network`, `afo-interest`, `newsletter` |

**Add privacy-friendly analytics (optional, free):**
- **Cloudflare Web Analytics** is free and has no cookie banner. Add the snippet only if the org wants it; it doesn't slow the site down.
- Once analytics is on, watch clicks on the **Donate** button, visits to **/become-a-coach**, and the **Thank you** page (`/thanks`), which every form submission lands on.

**Tag links you share** so you can tell which channel brought people. Add `?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=coach-recruiting` to the end of links in posts and emails.

## 8. Keeping the website fresh (5 minutes a month)

- Log in at **levelupcincinnati.org/admin** to add or edit **events**, coaches, scholars, partners, and stats.
- Events with a date move to "Past events" automatically the day after they happen. Just keep adding the next ones.
- Update the stats on the homepage and Become a Coach page each year when new results come in (Site data → Stats).
